Academic Search Tips:
- Use specific keywords.
- Filter by publication year.
- Read abstracts before downloading papers.
- Check citation counts.
- Review references for related work.

  - Introduction
  - Methods
  - Results

- Cite sources properly.
- Avoid plagiarism.
- Proofread before submission.
